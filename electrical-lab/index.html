Educational models, simplified on purpose. Code references are to the 2020 and 2023 editions of the National Electrical Code (NFPA 70), which most US states, Florida included, have adopted in one of those editions; rules change between editions and local amendments apply. Ampacities, sizes and densities are typical illustrative values. Real projects are designed by a licensed electrical engineer and approved by the authority having jurisdiction (AHJ).
