Sheet & Seam — how ductwork is laid out, cut, folded and joined

Theme
Drawings are schematic and exaggerated for clarity (metal thickness and seam gaps are drawn far larger than life). Allowances, gauges and spacings shown are typical illustrative values; real work follows the governing code, the project specification, SMACNA HVAC Duct Construction Standards (or EN 1505/1506 and DW/144 in Europe) and the settings of your own machines.
