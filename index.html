Learning · interactive study plates

Building systems, taken apart and animated

Self-built explainers drawn like plates from an engineering reference book. Click any part to see what it does, and run the scenarios to watch the system work.

Plate I

Air in Motion — a duct-design laboratory

Duct sizing, velocity, static pressure, fan and system curves, and branch balancing, all from a real network solution of a four-room supply system.
Plate II

Heat & Water — boiler, air handler and water heater

Every pipe, valve and valve type between an oil boiler, a hydro-air handler and an indirect water heater, plus the incoming water and the exhaust, with animated sequences of operation.
Plate III

Sheet & Seam — how ductwork is made

The terms, duct shapes, animated seams and joints, a fittings atlas, and a pattern shop that lays out flat patterns (elbows, gores, cones, square-to-rounds) with 1:1 SVG export.
Plate IV

Sun & Current — home solar and battery wiring

A Florida rooftop array, string or microinverters, with an optional battery and backup gateway. Every conductor from the modules to the meter, panel and loads, with live power flow through a day, a hurricane outage and a rapid shutdown.
Plate V

Watts & Wire — how a building's electrical system works

Volts, amps, ohms and watts; kW vs kWh; AC and three-phase; kVA and power factor; wire, breakers and voltage drop; grounding; one-line diagrams with generator transfer; panel schedules, load calcs, electrical rooms and plan symbols.
Plate VI

Mud & Tape — finishing gypsum board

Board types; setting (5, 20, 45, 90 minute) vs ready-mixed compound and when you can recoat; tape and bead; joints built coat by coat; the six levels of finish; a raking-light and paint-sheen simulator; primers, paint, textures, repairs and estimating.
Plate VII

Lumen & Lux — interior and exterior lighting design

Lumens, candelas, lux and luminance; colour temperature, CRI and spectra; lamps and LEDs; beams and photometry; layered interiors; a point-by-point room calculator; art aiming; daylight; dark-sky and turtle-safe exteriors; landscape techniques and 12 V voltage drop; dimming and controls.
Plate VIII

Sweep & Latch — door closers, arms and adjustment

How a hydraulic closer works, built around the LCN 4040XP: a moving cutaway; backcheck, main, latch and delay valves with the ADA 5-second rule; spring power and opening force; regular, top jamb and parallel arm geometry; all thirteen arms; plates and shoes; finishes and a spec builder; codes, handing and troubleshooting.
Plate IX

Plumb & Square — framing a wood house

The platform frame and its load paths; lumber and engineered wood; span tables rebuilt live; nails and connectors; layout; floors; a wall framer with headers and a cut list; bracing and wind; rafters; a hip and valley rafter calculator with a 3D roof; stairs.
Educational models, not design tools or construction documents. Everything runs in your browser; no data leaves the page.
