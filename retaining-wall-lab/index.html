Educational models, simplified on purpose. The stability checks use textbook Rankine and Coulomb earth pressure with presumptive soil values; they are not a design, and they skip internal, compound and global stability, seismic load and settlement. Code references (2021 IRC and IBC, ACI 318, OSHA 1926 Subpart P, ASTM C1372) summarise commonly adopted editions; the edition your state adopted, its amendments, local zoning and conservation rules, and the authority having jurisdiction govern. Frost depths are typical values: your building department sets the number. Walls over 4 ft, or holding up a driveway, a slope, a building or another wall, need a licensed engineer.
