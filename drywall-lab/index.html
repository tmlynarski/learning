Mud & Tape — finishing gypsum board

Theme
Educational models, simplified on purpose. Levels of finish follow the Gypsum Association's GA-214 (paraphrased); application follows GA-216 and ASTM C840; materials are covered by ASTM C1396 (board) and C475 (compound and tape). Set and dry times, shrinkage and coverage are typical illustrative values: the bag, the pail and the project specification govern. Fire-rated assemblies must be built and finished exactly as their tested design (UL or GA-600) says. Everything runs in your browser; no data leaves the page.
