Plumb & Square — framing a wood house

Theme
Educational models, simplified on purpose. Rules are summarised from the 2021 International Residential Code (IRC) chapters 3, 5, 6 and 8, the AWC National Design Specification (NDS) and Wood Frame Construction Manual (WFCM), and common trade practice; the Florida Building Code – Residential follows the IRC with added wind provisions. The code edition your jurisdiction adopted, the approved plans, the truss and engineered-lumber drawings and the connector maker's instructions govern. Spans here are computed, not copied from code tables: check them against the adopted tables or an engineer. Everything runs in your browser; no data leaves the page.
