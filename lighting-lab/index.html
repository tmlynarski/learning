Lumen & Lux — lighting and lighting design

Theme
Educational models, simplified on purpose. Recommended light levels are typical ranges after IES guidance (the IES Lighting Handbook and RP series); the project's criteria, the IES recommended practice for the application and the adopted energy code (IECC, ASHRAE/IES 90.1, California Title 24) govern. Exterior limits are paraphrased from the IES/IDA Model Lighting Ordinance and IES TM-15 (BUG ratings); wildlife guidance from the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission. Colour calculations use analytic fits to the CIE 1931 observer and modelled spectra, not measured lamp data. Everything runs in your browser; no data leaves the page.
